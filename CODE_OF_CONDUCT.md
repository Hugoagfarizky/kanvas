# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want Kanvas to be a welcoming place for anyone who wants to learn, build or teach. We pledge to
make participation harassment-free regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Ask questions when something is unclear instead of assuming bad intent.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other channel where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer,
[@Hugoagfarizky](https://github.com/Hugoagfarizky), through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed as
quickly as possible and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or
block accounts that violate this code.
